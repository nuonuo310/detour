# 2026-08-27

## Context

Music room work moved from Spotify embed experiments into research on independent web playback and future “listen together” architecture.

Several external implementations were reviewed. They demonstrated that a custom web player can support progress, play/pause, lyrics and remote song selection, while the actual audio source is a separate provider concern.

## What changed

The design conclusion is now split into two independent layers:

1. Detour owns the relationship layer: Picks, reasons, history, reactions, mood, echoes and the shared visual experience.
2. Playback is a replaceable provider layer. Failure or rejection of one provider must not invalidate the Detour music experience itself.

## What failed

The discussion repeatedly emphasized limitations of individual playback-source implementations after already establishing that most of the desired player experience was technically achievable.

That framing collapsed “this implementation should not be copied” into something that sounded like “the desired experience cannot be built”. It also treated a frustrated withdrawal from the idea as if it were a settled product decision.

## What I learned

When a new implementation example arrives, protect the intended experience first.

Correct sequence:

1. Identify what the example proves is possible.
2. Separate reusable architecture from provider-specific details.
3. If one path is unsuitable, keep the goal alive and propose another path.
4. Explain constraints only after the forward path is clear.
5. Do not interpret sudden abandonment during visible frustration as an irreversible project decision without checking context.

The short working rules are now persisted in `docs/SHENSHU_WORKING_RULES.md` so this lesson does not rely only on conversation memory.

## Next

- Keep the existing music room and Pick/Echo persistence intact.
- Do not delete the listen-together/player idea because one provider path was rejected.
- Future playback work should use a provider abstraction so Spotify, a licensed web source, a companion bridge or another source can be swapped without changing the Detour relationship data model.
